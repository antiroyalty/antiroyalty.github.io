Dimensions and working assumptions
Finished footprint
78″ × 44″ between finished faces; the curb is outside this area.
Ceiling
96″ (8′) above the shower floor at the center of the back wall, directly below the niche.
Custom niche
28″ wide × 20″ high clear opening, with 5″ usable depth. The finished front sill is 48″ above the shower floor directly below it; the opening top is 68″ above the same datum. The sill rises toward the back at ¼″ per foot. Cotone rows are set out to align a horizontal joint with the niche’s upper bullnose edge. Green wall band
8″ above the local sloped floor on three walls. Regular Cypress field tile meets the Sensi Terre Cotone tile in the same finished plane across a grout joint. No bullnose is used at this transition.
Floor and drain
¼″-per-foot main slope and wall-strip slope. Faceted end zones feed the drain ends. The 35 7⁄16″ × 2 29⁄32″ frame and solid grate are flush with the tile datum. The nearest frame edge is 2″ from the finished left wall.
Curb
4″ finished width, including tile, and 3″ above the outside bathroom floor. One regular 2″ × 4″ tile piece is cut to span the cap between the joints at both bullnose edges. Short-edge bullnose is on the inside and outside vertical faces, not a second row of cap tiles. The cap slopes inward at ¼″ per foot. Inside exposure varies with the floor slope. Michael must confirm the build-up, edge joints, and actual tile dimensions.
Elevation reference

Delivered sizes, grout width, cut layout, and trim profiles need installer confirmation. Bullnose is used only at the curb and niche, with individual tile joints.
