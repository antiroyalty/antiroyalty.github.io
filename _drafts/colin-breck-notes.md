It also helps to separate three different questions:
- Who owns the equipment?
- Who decides what gets built?
- Who controls it from moment to moment?


This is also the deeper issue in “How to electrify, fast.” California currently asks households to make bottom-up investments inside a system whose infrastructure, information, and approvals remain centrally controlled. A better arrangement would plan the shared grid from the top down while making ordinary electrification simple and flexible from the bottom up.
